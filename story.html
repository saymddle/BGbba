Our Story

A dream brewed from community, culture, and a deep love for good tea — and good people.

Every cup tells a story. Here's ours. 🧋
✦ The Beginning
It Started With A Dream
Black Girl Boba wasn't born in a boardroom — it was born in a kitchen, over laughter, late nights, and a lot of boba experimentation. What started as a passion for crafting the perfect cup of bubble tea grew into something so much bigger: a vision for a community-centered business that celebrates culture, creativity, and connection.
✦ The Mission
More Than Just Boba
From day one, we knew this was about more than tea. Every cup we make is an opportunity to create a moment — of joy, of belonging, of saying "you matter here." Augusta deserves a boba spot that feels like home, that reflects the richness and vibrancy of this city and its people. That's exactly what we set out to build.
We didn't just open a food truck.
We opened a space for community, creativity, and good energy.
— Founder, Black Girl Boba
✦ The Craft
Quality Is Non-Negotiable
We source real ingredients — fresh fruit, premium loose-leaf teas, quality milks and non-dairy alternatives — and brew everything in-house, fresh daily. No syrups from a concentrate. No shortcuts. We spent months perfecting our tapioca pearls, our sugar ratios, our flavor profiles. Because when you sip Black Girl Boba, we want you to taste the difference.
✦ The Future
Sip By Sip, We're Growing
What started as a food truck rolling through Augusta is just the beginning. We envision Black Girl Boba as a movement — expanding into a brick-and-mortar, hosting community events, partnering with local creatives and makers, and building a brand that Black girls (and everyone who loves them) can be proud of. The vibes are only getting better.
✦ What We Stand For ✦

Our Values

Community First
We show up for Augusta and Augusta shows up for us. Every dollar spent here builds something bigger together.
Real Ingredients
We refuse to compromise on quality. Real tea, real fruit, real milk — because you deserve the real thing.
Black Excellence
A celebration of Black creativity, entrepreneurship, and culture. We exist to inspire and make space.
Good Energy
From the way we greet you to the care in every cup, we pour good energy into everything we do.
